About me

I'm Tyler, a Computer Science student at CSU Monterey Bay who likes figuring out how software really works, usually by taking it apart.

01How I got here

I started with games. I wanted to know what was going on underneath, so I opened Fortnite's client in IDA and Cheat Engine and started mapping its memory and working out how its protections held up. That turned into hundreds of hours of reverse engineering, and the videos I made about it ended up with more than 10 million views across TikTok and YouTube.

Somewhere along the way I realized the part I liked most was finding the weak spot. So I pointed that at web apps. This past summer I interned remotely at NuggMD, a telehealth company with over 2 million patients, where I pen-tested their production platform with Burp Suite, worked through the OWASP Top 10, and found and fixed critical-severity access control bugs. Now I'm doing security work at Wagr, a skill-based betting app where real money is on the line.

I also like building things. In April my team of three won OtterHack 2026, CSUMB's AI hackathon, out of 21 teams with Study Buddy. That was my idea: an app that reads your heart rate from an ECG strap and tells you when you need a break. It's live at study-buddy.dev.

Before any of this, I spent two years in high school at NaturesTech, running a $5,000/month ad budget for their Shopify store and shipping 1,000+ orders a month. On campus I keep the 20+ machines in the CSUMB Esports Lounge running.
